Opportunity analysis, ownership tracking, personal finances, AI guidance, and education share one data model. You never rebuild the model twice.

Two states

Every asset is either a candidate or a holding.

The same record follows the asset from first look to ownership. After closing, your assumptions become the baseline that actual results are measured against.

Every important number in AIM shows where it came from. You always know whether a value is your assumption, an actual you entered, or data from a connected account.

The AIM loop

From first idea to the next decision.

AIM stays useful through the entire ownership cycle, not only on the day you run a calculator.

  1. Analyze

    Model the opportunity with transparent assumptions and downside cases.

  2. Decide

    See the risks, the assumptions that drive the result, and a clear recommendation.

  3. Own

    Convert the analysis into a holding without reentering anything.

  4. Improve

    Track actuals against plan, get alerts, and act on the biggest gaps.

  5. Learn

    AIM Learning teaches each concept inside the model where you use it.
