FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Who is AIM for?

People who want to own income producing assets without a finance team behind them: first time rental buyers, landlords and hosts, people buying a small business, and anyone building the savings to do one of those.

Does AIM tell me what to buy?

No. AIM shows you whether a deal meets the targets you set, what could go wrong, and what price would make it work. The decision stays yours. AIM provides analysis and education, not investment, tax, or legal advice.

Where do the numbers come from?

From you, during the beta. Every figure is either an assumption you entered or an actual you logged, and AIM labels which is which. Bank and listing connections come later in the roadmap.

Is my data private?

During the beta your workspace is saved in your own browser and is not sent to AIM servers. Your demo request details are used only to contact you about AIM.

What does it cost?

The beta is free. AIM Core will be $15 a month at launch, and beta members get early pricing.

How is the AIM Score calculated?

It weighs return against your target, debt coverage, how the deal holds up in a downside case, whether it fits your available cash, and the quality of the assumptions. The score is a summary. The numbers behind it are always one tap away.
