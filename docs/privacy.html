Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

AIM (Artemis Investment Metrics) is operated by Carlisle Capital LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the AIM website and the AIM beta workspace, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Sign up details. When you get started we collect your name, email address, the description you choose, the asset types you are interested in, and optionally how many assets you own.
  • Your workspace. During the beta, the figures you enter into AIM (deals, budgets, accounts, and goals) are saved in your own browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to our servers. The live demo is a separate workspace of sample data, also stored only in your browser.
  • Share links. If you copy a share link for an analysis, that analysis's name, location, and assumptions are encoded in the link itself. Anyone you give the link to can read them. We do not store shared links.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback from inside AIM, we receive your message along with your name and email if you provided them.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested in order to operate and secure the site.

How we use it

  • To give you access to the AIM workspace and to contact you about the beta.
  • To improve AIM based on what beta users ask for.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect the security of the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to host the site and to receive sign up and feedback forms. They process data only on our behalf and under their own security and privacy commitments.

Your choices

You can clear your AIM workspace at any time from Settings, or by clearing your browser's site data. To ask us to delete your sign up details, reply to any email you receive from AIM and we will remove them.

Children

AIM is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
